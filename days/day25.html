--- Day 25: Delivery Dilemma ---

By selina

Christmas Eve has finally arrived, but the sleigh can only hold so much weight. Santa and the elves need your help to find how many rounds it takes to deliver all the presents before families wake to empty houses.

Your manager, Selina, has assigned you to New York. Santa has 10,000 gifts to deliver within an hour before moving on to another state and country. You are the elves' last hope!

--- Load the sleigh ---

The sleigh can carry at most 10,000 lbs and 10,000 volume units per round. Each line describes one gift as weight-volume:

120-468

This gift weighs 120 lbs and occupies 468 volume units.

  • Load gifts in their input order. Do not reorder or skip ahead.
  • If the next gift would make either total exceed 10,000, deliver the current load and start a new round with that gift.
  • A total of exactly 10,000 is allowed. Every gift fits individually.
  • Both totals reset for each round. Count the final round, even if it is only partly full.
