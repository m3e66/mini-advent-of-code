--- Day 68: Christmas Tree Delivery ---

By Jareth

Giving out Christmas presents isn't free, so to help Santa pay for all of the gifts he delivers each year, the elves are opening a Christmas tree delivery program! Instead of people having to buy a tree and decorate it themselves, they can place their orders online to have a pre-decorated tree delivered to their house. However, the elves need your help to determine how much to charge for their services.

Each customer's order is represented by a tree diagram. A blank line separates one tree from the next:

--- Price each order ---

  • The base cost is the tree's height × $50. Height is the number of nonblank rows in that tree.
  • Each normal ornament (o) adds $2.
  • Each roll of tinsel (@) adds $5.
  • A tree with more than 10 rolls of tinsel incurs one $75 surcharge. Exactly 10 rolls do not trigger it.
  • Add a 20% markup to that tree's entire cost, including any surcharge.

Stars (*) and indentation add no decoration costs. Blank separator lines do not add height. Calculate each tree separately, then add its marked-up price to the batch total.

--- Example order ---

       *
      *o*
     **o*@
    *ooo***
   **@*oo*o@
  @@*o**ooo@*
 *@*o*@ooo*o**
**o@oo@o***o@oo

This tree has 8 rows, 24 normal ornaments, and 11 rolls of tinsel.

Its cost is 8 × $50 + 24 × $2 + 11 × $5 + $75 = $578. The tinsel surcharge applies because 11 is greater than 10.

The markup is $578 × 20% = $115.60. The elves charge $578 + $115.60 = $693.60 for this order.

How much money will this batch of customer orders make the elves? Submit the total sales amount in dollars, including all costs and markup, such as 693.60. Use at most two decimal places, with no dollar sign or commas.
