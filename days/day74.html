--- Day 74: Uncle Bob kindly sponsored this feast ---

By ada

A big family has come together to celebrate Christmas by having a huge pot-luck feast together. The family is eating at Uncle Bob's huge mansion.

The foods on offer are TURKEY, BREAD, SALAD, HAM, SAUSAGE, PIZZA, EGG, DRUMSTICK, BEEF, and LASAGNA.

Each person eats four different foods. However, not long after the feast, Little Bobby junior becomes sick. The family discovers that four foods—PIZZA, EGG, SALAD, and HAM—were prepared poorly.

The sick family members must go to the hospital, and nobody has insurance. Uncle Bob has kindly agreed to pay their bills.

--- Feast rules ---

Use these rules for this puzzle:

  • A vaccinated guest becomes sick after eating at least 5 servings of poisoned food.
  • An unvaccinated guest becomes sick after eating at least 3 servings of poisoned food.
  • Add poisoned servings across all four foods; the threshold is not per dish.
  • Guests are aged 10 through 99, inclusive.
  • A sick minor, aged 10–17, costs $528. A sick adult, aged 18–99, costs $1,647.
  • Each guest eats four distinct foods, with 1–4 servings of each. Different guests may eat the same foods.

Charge one bill per sick guest. Guests who do not become sick add nothing to the total.

--- Decode the guest list ---

Your input is one comma-separated list. The first digit of each record is vaccination status: 1 for vaccinated or 0 for unvaccinated. The next two digits give the guest's age. Then come four uppercase food names, each immediately followed by its serving count.

016TURKEY2BREAD3SALAD2HAM3,120SAUSAGE4PIZZA2EGG4DRUMSTICK3

The first guest is unvaccinated and 16 years old. They eat 2 + 3 = 5 poisoned servings from SALAD and HAM. This meets the threshold of 3, so their hospital bill is $528.

The second guest is vaccinated and 20 years old. They eat 2 + 4 = 6 poisoned servings from PIZZA and EGG. This meets the threshold of 5, so their bill is $1,647.

Uncle Bob pays $528 + $1,647 = $2,175 for this example.

How much must Uncle Bob pay for all sick guests in your input? Submit a whole number of dollars, such as 2175, without a dollar sign or commas.
