--- A small coding tradition ---

Mini Advent of Code is a free, community-made collection of Christmas-themed programming problems. We love the creativity of our contributors and the many different ways you can solve their puzzles.

Inspired by Advent of Code. This is an independent fan project, not affiliated with the original event.
